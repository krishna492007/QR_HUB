Privacy Policy for QR HUB

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

Welcome to QR HUB ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Krishna Tech Hub. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information.

1. Information We Access

QR HUB is designed to operate primarily as an offline utility tool. We only request permissions that are strictly necessary for core app functionality:

  • Camera Permission: Used exclusively in real-time to scan QR codes and product barcodes via Google ML Kit computer vision. No camera frames or video streams are recorded, stored, or transmitted to any external server.
  • Storage / Media Access: Used only when you explicitly choose to scan a QR image from your photo gallery or save generated QR codes and printable PDF sticker sheets to your device storage.

2. Data Storage & Privacy

All scanned and generated history is stored locally in your device's private SQLite database (Room DB). We do not operate proprietary tracking servers, and your scan history never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Services & Ads

Our app may display advertisements provided by trusted advertising networks (such as Start.io / Google AdMob) to support development. These third-party services may collect anonymous device diagnostic information (such as Advertising ID) in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.
