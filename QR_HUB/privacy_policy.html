Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy for QR HUB

Effective Date: August 2026 | App Version: 1.0.0

1. Overview & Commitment

Welcome to QR HUB, developed by Krishna. Your privacy is our highest priority. QR HUB is designed as an offline-first, privacy-respecting utility app.

We do NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, camera feeds, scan history, or payment details to any external server.

2. Permissions Used & Why

  • CAMERA Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely for real-time optical scanning of QR codes and barcodes using on-device machine learning (Google ML Kit). Camera video is processed locally in RAM and is never recorded, captured to disk, or sent over the internet.
  • STORAGE / PHOTOS Permission (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Used only when you choose to scan a QR code image from your gallery, or export/save generated QR codes to your device's Downloads/Pictures directory.
  • VIBRATE Permission (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used to provide subtle haptic tactile feedback when a QR code is successfully detected.
  • INTERNET Permission: Used solely if you tap on a URL link from a scanned QR code to open it in your default web browser.

3. UPI & Financial Transactions

QR HUB provides convenient UPI QR code parsing. When you scan a UPI payment code (e.g. PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM):

  • QR HUB only extracts standard UPI URI parameters (Payee VPA, Payee Name, Amount).
  • The payment is handed over directly to your official installed banking app via secure Android App Intents.
  • QR HUB never accesses, collects, or stores your bank accounts, UPI PIN, passwords, or transaction records.

4. Local Data Storage

All your scan history, favorite items, and generated QR codes are stored strictly locally on your device using an encrypted local SQLite/Room database. You can clear or export your history at any time from the History menu.

5. Third-Party Services

QR HUB utilizes on-device Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning. Google ML Kit processes barcode data locally on your device hardware without sending data to Google servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. The app is completely safe for all age groups.
