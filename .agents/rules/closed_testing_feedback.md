# Google Play Closed Testing Feedback & Production Q&A Tracker

## Rule Objective
Whenever the user shares screenshots or text of tester feedback, reviews, bug reports, or suggestions during the 14-day closed testing phase:

1. **Log the Feedback**: Save every tester review, device model, Android version, date, and rating into C:\Users\Admin\Desktop\PlayStore_Assets\TESTER_FEEDBACK_LOG.md.
2. **Action & Fix**: Analyze if any code/UI adjustment is needed, plan the fix, and batch it for the Version 1.1 production release.
3. **Production Questionnaire Preparation**: Automatically organize the feedback and resolution details to answer Google Play's mandatory production access questionnaire:
   - *Question 1:* How did you recruit your testers?
   - *Question 2:* How did testers test your app and what feedback did they give?
   - *Question 3:* What changes or improvements did you make based on their feedback?
   - *Question 4:* How did you decide that your app is ready for production?

## Style & Communication
- Respond in Roman Hindi / Hinglish only (strictly no Devanagari Hindi).
- Maintain 100% data integrity of Keystore credentials, AdMob IDs, and Play Store assets.
